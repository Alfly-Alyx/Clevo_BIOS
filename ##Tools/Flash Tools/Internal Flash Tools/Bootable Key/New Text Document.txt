Format the flash drive via Rufus in FreeDOS, and upload the contents of the archive to the root of the flash drive
1) Boot into the BIOS, in the Security section, disable Secure Boot (disabled by default)
2) Boot from a flash drive in UEFI mode, enter the meset command (the fans will work at 100% and the laptop will reboot)
3) Boot again from the flash drive, enter the fpt -d backup.bin command to save the current BIOS to backup.bin
4 TM) Enter the fpt -f p751tm0511.bin -rewrite -y command, or fpt -f p751tm0729.bin -rewrite -y   FOR RTX videocards, wait for the end of the process (erasing, flashing, verification) 
      and enter the fpt -greset command (the laptop will reboot with clearing CMOS)
5) To roll back to the previous BIOS, repeat steps 2 and 4, changing the command to fpt -f backup.bin -rewrite -y
