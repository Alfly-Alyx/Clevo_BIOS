Format the flash drive via Rufus in FreeDOS, and upload the contents of the archive to the root of the flash drive

Place your new bios, and name it "bios.rom"

1) Boot into the BIOS, in the Security section, disable Secure Boot.
2) Boot from the flash drive into the UEFI Shell, enter the "meset" command (the fans will spin at 100% and the laptop will reboot. This unlocks the write access to all flash regions until the next full shutdown).
3) Boot again from the flash drive into the UEFI Shell, enter the "fpt -d backup.bin" command to save the current full BIOS.
4) Enter the flashing command:
   -> To flash EVERYTHING (including ME and GbE): "fpt -f bios.rom -rewrite -y" (Requires step 2 to be successful)
   -> To flash ONLY the BIOS region: "fpt -f bios.rom -bios -y" (Safer, preserves your MAC address)
   Wait for the end of the process (erasing, flashing, verification).
5) Enter the "fpt -greset" command (the laptop will reboot and global reset/CMOS clear will be performed).

<) To roll back to the previous BIOS, repeat steps 2, 3 and 4, changing the command to "fpt -f backup.bin -rewrite -y".
